This text provides course instructions regarding required reading for a deep learning course.

The core reading for Part 2 of the course is *Deep learning with Python*, authored by F. Chollet and M. Watson (2025), specifically the third edition ebook published by Manning Publications. To access this essential text, students must consult the specific instructions available on the Manning Publications website, found in the PDF file named `Manning_Chollet 3e_instructions.pdf`.

Additionally, the older text, *Deep learning with Python* (2017) by F. Chollett (first edition), is listed as supplemental further reading for Part 1 of the course. This earlier edition is available to students within the Online Library section of the O'Reilly Collection.

In summary, the course divides its required material into two parts, correlating specific editions of Chollett's deep learning texts with two different access methods (Manning Publications site and O'Reilly Online Library).

# 1.101 Applications of Machine Learning
Video • 6 min - Summary

URL: https://www.coursera.org/learn/uol-cm3015-machine-learning-and-neural-networks/lecture/DEMDo/1-101-applications-of-machine-learning

This course provides a comprehensive, twenty-week deep dive into the fundamentals of machine learning and neural networks. The initial weeks focus on setting up the foundation, covering the course syllabus and introducing key resources like Chollet and Watson's *Deep Learning with Python*. Early topics explore the diverse applications and fundamental types of machine learning, including understanding the conceptual limitations of the machine learning "black box." Specific attention is paid to the notation and principles of Supervised learning before moving into core problem types.

Weeks are dedicated sequentially to mastering Classification, which is followed by detailed instruction in Regression. Subsequent modules address critical practical aspects, including Model Evaluation and improvement techniques, and introduce advanced methods such as Probabilistic classification. The course then shifts focus to Unsupervised learning, where methods for discovering patterns without labeled data are extensively covered.

A major transition occurs with the introduction of Neural Networks and Deep Learning, becoming the central focus of the curriculum. A significant portion of the later weeks is dedicated to studying the mathematical building blocks of these networks, providing the underlying theoretical rigor. This deep mathematical exploration is complemented by practical sessions focused on getting started with implementing neural networks.

The final weeks synthesize these complex topics, consolidating the fundamentals of machine learning and providing a holistic view of modern AI applications. Overall, the structure progresses from basic definitions and types to complex mathematical architectures and practical implementation.

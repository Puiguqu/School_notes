# 5.202 Further reading
Reading • 1h - Summary

URL: https://www.coursera.org/learn/uol-cm3015-machine-learning-and-neural-networks/supplement/xj53P/5-202-further-reading

The content of this specific topic is described as being largely self-contained. For a supplementary resource, the course textbook authored by Chollet includes a small dedicated section on Naive Bayes, found under section 1.2.1. When seeking further reading on Probabilistic modelling, students are directed to the work of Ethem Alpaydin. Specifically, Alpaydin's book, *Introduction to machine learning*, covers this subject in sections 3.1 and 3.2. The publication details for this text are: Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 2014, and it is the 3rd edition, bearing ISBN 9780262028189. An extensive resource for deep study on probabilistic modelling is offered by K. Murphy. Murphy's volume is titled *Machine learning: a probabilistic perspective*. The publication details for Murphy's book are: Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 2012, and its ISBN is 9780262018029. For a strong introductory perspective on the current topic, the beginning of Chapter 2 of Alpaydin's book is recommended. This initial material covers sections 2.1 to 2.2, providing a good introduction to probabilistic modelling concepts. Students are encouraged to practice by attempting the exercises numbered 2.1 through 2.9. Generative modelling and Bayes are covered in the beginning of Chapter 3 of Alpaydin’s work. This coverage specifically includes sections 3.1 through 3.2.4. To read about Naïve Bayes specifically, one can skip to section 3.5 of Alpaydin’s book.
